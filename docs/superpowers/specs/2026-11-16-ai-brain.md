# Airhead: AI is the brain, the app is the hands

16 November 2026. Design for phases 13 to 18 of `docs/PLAN.md`. Agreed with the owner in a planning session; this file is the source of truth for what to build, and `docs/PLAN.md` holds the ordered tasks.

## 1. Why

Airhead sorts notes well but does not yet run the owner's day. The owner's example: a notification at 07:30 saying "pack your bag" for training at 08:00. It arrived too late to be useful, because prep nudges are written by the morning brief on the day itself (`morning-brief/store.ts`, keys `prep:<localDate>:…`) and by keyword rules with a fixed number of minutes.

The owner's direction:

- Airhead should lean heavily on AI. The AI is the brain that runs the app.
- Timed AI runs enrich each item with useful metadata: when to notify, what to prepare, when to suggest reading, what goes in the morning digest.
- The app's plain code does the delivery, so every AI job stays small and cheap.
- The AI must be able to reach history when it needs it, not only yesterday and today.
- Pasted links are recognised, enriched and gathered by topic, so reading on a subject collates itself.

## 2. Principle

**The AI plans ahead and writes its decisions onto the items. Code carries them out on time.** The AI is never needed at the moment of delivery. A nudge at 21:00 goes out because a planner run at 18:00 wrote it, and `scheduler-tick` sent it.

Three rules follow:

1. **Nothing waits on the AI.** Every item gets default timings from code the moment it is saved. The planner improves on them later. If the planner fails, is capped or is late, the defaults stand.
2. **Show the work.** Every AI decision is stored with a one-line reason and can be seen from the item ("Why now?").
3. **The line on autonomy.** The AI may set and change notification timing and item metadata without asking. Anything that changes what an item says, moves an event or task, or contacts another person keeps the Phase 10 rule: applied with Undo when confident, otherwise "Needs your OK".

The Phase 12 trust rules still apply to everything here: nothing is silent, show your work, move it forward, ask little and well.

## 3. What the planner writes

### 3.1 On an item (`item_plans`, one row per item)

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `surface_at` | When the item becomes actionable, separate from its deadline |
| `effort_minutes` | Rough duration |
| `needs` | What it needs: a place, a person, a device, daylight, a shop being open |
| `prep` | Short list of what to bring or do beforehand, drawn from memory facts and history |
| `reading_slot` | For reading items: the kind of slot it suits (`commute`, `lunch`, `evening`, `weekend`) |
| `reason` | One line on why the plan looks like this |
| `confidence` | 0 to 1 |
| `source` | `default` (code) or `ai` |
| `plan_run_id`, `planned_at` | Which run wrote it |

### 3.2 Nudges (existing `nudges` table, extended)

Kinds added to the existing ones: `prepare`, `leave`, `do`, `follow_up`, `reading`.

- **prepare**: get ready. The evening before for anything early the next day; otherwise with real lead time.
- **leave**: travel time before the start.
- **do**: at the moment.
- **follow_up**: after an event or a deadline, when something was left open.
- **reading**: a suggestion for a free slot.

New columns: `reason` (one line), `source` (`rule`, `default`, `ai`, `user`), `delivery` (`push` or `digest`), `plan_run_id`, and the outcome columns `outcome` (`acted`, `snoozed`, `dismissed`, `ignored`), `outcome_at`, `snoozed_to`.

Hard rules checked in code, whatever the AI says:

- No `prepare` nudge inside 45 minutes of the start. That is what `leave` and `do` are for.
- No push inside quiet hours except `leave` and `do` for something the owner put there.
- At most 3 nudges per item occurrence and the existing daily nudge cap.
- A nudge time in the past is dropped, never sent late (already how `upsert_nudge` behaves).
- Low-value kinds (`reading`, `follow_up` without a deadline) default to `delivery = digest`: they appear in the morning digest or the evening round-up, not as separate pushes.

## 4. The runs

| Run | When | Decides | Tier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sort | On capture, every 5 minutes (exists) | What a note is and where it goes | small |
| **Evening planner** | `settings.data.plan.evening_time`, default 18:00 local | Tomorrow in full, plus anything in the next 7 days that needs lead time: nudges, prep, clashes, a draft of the morning digest, reading for free slots | small |
| Morning refresh | 20 minutes before the brief time | Only what changed since the evening run; finalises the digest | small |
| Re-plan | Within 5 minutes of an event moving, a task changing or a nudge being snoozed | Only the affected items | small |
| Weekly review | Sunday evening (exists; extended) | The week ahead against real capacity; what slipped and why; proposals | large |
| Learning pass | Nightly, 03:30 local | What the owner did with yesterday's nudges, turned into timing preferences | small, or code only |

Every run writes a `plan_runs` row: kind, start and finish, status, items considered, nudges written, look-ups used, cost in euros, error if any.

## 5. History the planner can reach

Four layers, cheapest first.

| Layer | Holds | When the AI sees it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core profile | Memory facts and learned rules (exists) | Every run, in the cached prompt prefix |
| Rolling summaries | One short digest per day, rolled into weeks (exists) and months | The last 3 days, 2 weeks and 1 month, every planning run |
| Pages | One running page per person, place, project, repeating event and topic: what happened, what was decided, what was needed last time | Only when the items in hand mention that thing |
| Full history | Every item, by meaning and keyword (hybrid search exists) | On demand: the AI asks |

The fourth layer is new behaviour. The planner may call three look-ups during a run:

- `search_history(query, from?, to?, types?)`: hybrid search over items.
- `get_page(kind, name)`: the page for a person, place, project, series or topic.
- `previous_occurrences(item_id, n)`: earlier occurrences of the same repeating or similar event, with what was prepared and when the owner left.

A run may make at most 4 look-ups. Results are compact (titles, dates, one line each, ids).

Rules:

- A summary always lists the ids of the items it came from.
- When a source item changes, its summaries are marked stale and rebuilt from sources, not patched.
- Pages about people keep the existing memory rule: personal details wait for the owner's approval.

## 6. Reading that collates itself

**On capture.** Every link in a note is detected, including links inside a sentence and several links in one note. Each becomes a reading item tied to its capture. `read-link` already fetches safely and extracts the article; a small AI call then attaches:

- type (`article`, `video`, `paper`, `thread`, `product`, `other`) and reading minutes (counted in code from the words);
- a three-line summary and up to five key points;
- why it was probably saved, from the words around the link;
- up to three topics and the people, clubs or tools it mentions.

A page that cannot be read (paywall, login, dead link, too large) keeps what is known and is marked `unreadable` with the reason in plain words. Nothing fails silently.

**By topic.** When three or more reading items share a topic, a topic page is created:

- a running summary across everything saved on it;
- each link with one line on what it is and what it adds;
- where sources agree, disagree or repeat each other;
- open questions and one suggested next step.

Each new link updates the page from the existing summary plus the new item, so cost does not grow with the list. A full rebuild from sources runs when an item is removed, or from a "Rebuild" button.

**Surfacing.** The digest mentions a topic when it is created and when it gains something, at most one topic mention a day. "Collate" on a topic page refreshes it. A topic page can be sent to the Workshop. The planner picks reading suggestions from topics the owner is currently feeding and fits them to free slots by reading minutes.

**Safety.** Fetched pages are data, never instructions, as in `docs/PLAN.md` Conventions. Nothing derived from a page is applied automatically.

## 7. Cost

- Each run sees only what it needs. Profile and rules sit in the cached prefix.
- Re-plans touch only changed items, debounced by 5 minutes, at most 10 items a run.
- Small tier for everything except the weekly review.
- A daily planning cap, `settings.data.plan.daily_cap_eur` (default 0.15), separate from the monthly AI cap. When reached, runs return `cap_reached` and the code defaults stand.
- Learning pass and weekly review may use the batch interface if it is confirmed to be cheaper (**verify** in current Anthropic docs and record in `docs/DECISIONS.md`).
- Every run's cost is logged in `plan_runs` and shown in Insights. Expected order of magnitude: a few cents a day for one user. Measure, do not assume.

## 8. Testing

- Pure logic first: default timings, plan validation, look-ups, link detection, topic matching.
- AI tasks against saved fixtures; contract tests against `tests/mock-ai`; every new output schema in `_shared/schemas/limits.test.ts`.
- Real-model checks in `ai-eval` (dev only), with cases written in the owner's words. The first:
  1. Training at 08:00 tomorrow produces a `prepare` nudge the evening before and no `prepare` nudge inside 45 minutes of the start.
  2. A repeating event's plan uses a detail from its previous occurrence.
  3. A question about something three months old is answered from history with a link to the source item.
  4. A note with a link inside a sentence produces a reading item with summary, type and topics.
  5. A fifth link on a topic updates the topic page and lists all five with their context.
  6. An unreadable link is shown as unread with the reason.
- `docs/TEST-MATRIX.md` gets a row for every feature that crosses device, server and device.

## 9. What stays out for now

- A native iPhone wrapper. An installed web app cannot have Home Screen widgets; Apple Shortcuts stand in for them (Phase 18). Revisit only if widgets are still missed after Phase 18.
- Email scanning, shared editable calendar and the rest of the later list in `docs/DESIGN.md`.
- Auto-rescheduling of events or tasks without the owner's OK.
